1.5 · Scaling

AspectToday
Unit of isolationOne chat group maps to one folder, one session UUID, one IPC namespace, and at most one running container.
ConcurrencyMAX_CONCURRENT_CONTAINERS defaults to 5. Additional groups remain in their independent queues.
Shared componentsAll groups share the host's Docker daemon, copilot-api, image cache, credential stores, NanoClaw process, and SQLite database.
What scales naturallyWorkspaces, IPC directories, and Claude session directories are already partitioned by group.
Single-host boundaryRouting state and session registry live in local SQLite; workspaces and transcripts live on the VM disk.
Multi-VM pathExternalize queueing, session registry, and durable storage; then assign a group to one worker at a time with a lease. See the "Walkthroughs" section for a full proposed architecture and end-to-end traces.
